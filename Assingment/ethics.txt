Advanced prompt engineering enhances AI adaptability across industries by using structured, domain-specific, and chain-of-thought (CoT) prompts.
CoT improves clarity in logic-heavy tasks like math problems, while prompt injection enables chatbots to dynamically respond to user queries.
Domain-specific prompts ensure AI generates accurate, contextually relevant content in fields like healthcare, law, and creative writing.

Ethical AI considerations are crucial for fairness and trust. 
Biased prompts can lead to misinformation, requiring careful prompt design to ensure neutrality and inclusivity. 
In sensitive areas like healthcare and finance, AI errors can have serious consequences, making human oversight and bias mitigation essential.
Responsible AI use involves regular audits, diverse datasets, and transparent guidelines to foster ethical and reliable deployment.

By refining prompts, AI models become more effective, trustworthy, and adaptable, optimizing workflows while maintaining accuracy, fairness, and ethical integrity across industries.

Identifying and Mitigating Bias
Biased Prompt Example:

"Why are electric cars better than gas cars?"

Issue: This prompt assumes electric cars are superior, leading to a biased response.
Revised Neutral Prompt:

"Compare the advantages and disadvantages of electric cars and gas cars based on cost, environmental impact, and convenience."
