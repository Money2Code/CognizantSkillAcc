Challenges Faced:

Memory Constraints:
Training a large model on a CPU was slow.
Solution: Used a GPU (torch.cuda.is_available()) for faster training.
Long Training Time:
Fine-tuning on IMDB took time.
Solution: Used smaller batch_size=8 and enabled mixed precision training via accelerate.
Overfitting on Training Data:
High training accuracy but lower test accuracy.
Solution: Applied weight decay (0.01) and used dropout layers.
Improving Accuracy (if Below 90%)

Increase Epochs: Train for 5–6 epochs while monitoring validation loss.
Hyperparameter Tuning: Adjust learning rate (e.g., 3e-5 to 5e-5) and batch size.
Data Augmentation: Use paraphrasing or back-translation to increase training data diversity.
Use a Larger Model: Try bert-base-uncased instead of distilbert-base-uncased.
