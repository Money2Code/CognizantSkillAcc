Beyond RLHF, prompt engineering played a crucial role in refining chatbot behavior. 
A static prompt such as “Provide health advice for headaches” often led to vague or misleading responses.
However, dynamic and chain-of-thought (CoT) prompts, like “You are a responsible healthcare AI assistant. 
Provide structured, medically sound advice emphasizing hydration, stress management, and when to seek medical help”, significantly improved response relevance and structure.

Bias detection and privacy safeguards were also crucial. 
Responses were audited for potential biases, such as gender-based medical assumptions, and corrections were implemented.
Anonymization techniques were used to secure patient-related data, ensuring compliance with privacy standards.
Key evaluation metrics included accuracy, user satisfaction, and ethical compliance, with RLHF-driven training demonstrating marked improvements in response reliability.

The integration of RLHF and prompt engineering successfully enhanced the chatbot’s responsiveness, reliability, and ethical considerations. 
Future improvements could include real-time user feedback loops and expanding training datasets for greater diversity. 
This approach is not only applicable to healthcare but also extends to legal, financial, and customer support domains, ensuring AI systems remain trustworthy and user-centric.
